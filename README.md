I'm interested in quantitative finance: how mathematical models describe markets,
where their assumptions break down, and how to test them with data. Most of my
work here is in Python, with a focus on portfolio analysis, econometrics and
numerical methods.

I'm currently studying cointegration and market-neutral pairs trading, with a
vectorized backtesting engine as my next project.

### Projects

[**Portfolio Optimizer**](https://github.com/alexdmngz/portfolio-optimizer)

Import holdings and compare allocations using historical returns, covariance and
Monte Carlo sampling. Includes a local Streamlit app and an offline demo.

[**Retrieval methods for technical Q&A**](https://github.com/alexdmngz/rag-mpe-benchmark)

A study of BM25, dense and hybrid retrieval across 70 questions about a research
paper. Developed with Shadi Fedriani Abdallah; finalist in the 2025/26
*Modelización de Problemas de la Empresa* contest.

### Tools

Python, R and SQL. NumPy, Pandas and SciPy for numerical work; Matplotlib for
visualization. My retrieval work also uses Gemini, ChromaDB and Sentence
Transformers.
